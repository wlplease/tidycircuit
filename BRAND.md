# TidyCircuit brand

**Position:** Calm, capable PC care. The product explains what it sees and gives people control over what changes.

## Identity

- Name: **TidyCircuit** (working name; formal trademark clearance pending).
- Tagline: **A clearer view of your PC. A calmer way to clean.**
- Voice: plain, specific, reassuring. Say “review this file,” never “fix dangerous junk.”
- Logo: three horizontal circuit traces, paired with warm nodes. The icon is supplied as `assets/icon.svg`; the horizontal lockup is `assets/wordmark.svg`.
- Logo clearance: original project artwork, created for this repository. The SVG files may be used in the app and project materials.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep ocean | `#143E50` | Primary text, logo ground |
| Lagoon | `#096D78` | Primary actions |
| Sea glass | `#7EE0D1` | Circuit traces, highlights |
| Sail cloth | `#F4F7F3` | Page background |
| Miami coral | `#F37E69` | Small accents and status details |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and working surfaces |

Use coral sparingly; ocean blue carries the main hierarchy. Never use color alone to explain a cleanup risk.
